Sky and ambient light

Torches, fires, lava, crystals, glowing terminals and any light a mini carries add light on top, and walls cast shadows. Every square is bright, dim or dark, and heroes need darkvision to see into darkness. Use No light for caves and sealed buildings.

Animated effects

Shimmering water with light ripples and foam, flowing lava, flickering firelight, weather, writhing tentacles and richer ground textures. Turn off on slower devices.
